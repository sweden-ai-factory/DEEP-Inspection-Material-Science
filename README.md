📖 **Lesson website:** https://learn.swedenaifactory.se/DEEP-Inspection-Material-Science/

---

## About

Inspection and characterization of materials underpin quality control, materials development and advanced manufacturing, but conventional image analysis relies heavily on expert time and is hard to scale. Deep learning offers a way to automate this by learning visual features directly from data — for defect classification, microstructure recognition, process monitoring and quality assessment.

### Who is it for?

Practitioners and researchers in materials science and industrial inspection who want to apply deep learning to surface defect analysis.
